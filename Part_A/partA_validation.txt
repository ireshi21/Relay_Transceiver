PART A FUNCTIONAL VALIDATION
Frame: 16 ID + 976 payload + 8 CRC = 1000 bits; 500 QPSK symbols.
Ideal test: 0 payload errors across 97600 bits; 100/100 CRC pass.
Entire frames and forwarded frames recovered exactly.
Separate CRC test: one injected bit error; frame 1 rejected; 99 other frames forwarded.
Default energy: 250 source + 250 relay = 500 per attempted frame.
CRC rejection uses 250 units; 250 units remain unused.
25/75, 50/50 and 75/25 energy splits passed per-frame budget checks.
No channel, destination combining or link-performance metrics are modeled.
