PART A: TRANSMITTER DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION
Cooperative Relay Transceiver with Direct, Relay and Combined Reception

Use these notes for your handwritten section. Insert the four exported plots,
copy the allocation table, and add your name and signature to every page.

1. SYSTEM AND PROTOCOL
Phase 1: the source broadcasts a QPSK frame to relay and destination.
Phase 2: the source is silent; the relay transmits a regenerated frame if CRC
passes, otherwise it remains silent. Both slots are reserved, even on failure.
Each slot lasts 500 normalized symbol periods. Frame boundaries and coherent
equalized symbols at the relay input are assumed available.
Draw: Source -> Relay -> Destination, with a direct Source -> Destination link.
Label both source links phase 1 and the relay-destination link phase 2.

2. FRAMING AND MODULATION
The seed is 16 using MATLAB's twister generator. We generate 100 frames.
Frame format: [16-bit frame ID | 976 payload bits | 8-bit CRC].
IDs run from 1 to 100 and are transmitted most significant bit first.
The ID provides frame identification, not timing synchronization.
Total length is 1000 bits, or 500 QPSK symbols per frame.
CRC covers the frame ID and payload. Its polynomial is x^8+x^2+x+1 (0x07),
with zero initial state, no reflection and no final XOR. The binary division
appends eight zeros and transmits the resulting eight-bit remainder.
Mapping: s=[(1-2*b1)+j*(1-2*b2)]/sqrt(2).
00 -> (1+j)/sqrt(2); 01 -> (1-j)/sqrt(2);
10 -> (-1+j)/sqrt(2); 11 -> (-1-j)/sqrt(2).
Each unscaled symbol has energy 1. Insert partA_payload.png and
partA_constellation.png.

3. ENERGY AND POWER
The maximum energy per attempted frame is 500 normalized units across the
two phases. Es=alpha*Etotal and Er=(1-alpha)*Etotal.
With symbol duration Ts=1 and N=500 symbols, Ps=Es/N and Pr=Er/N.
Source symbols are multiplied by sqrt(Ps), relay symbols by sqrt(Pr).

Source share alpha | Source energy | Relay energy | Source power | Relay power
0.25               | 125           | 375          | 0.25         | 0.75
0.50 (default)     | 250           | 250          | 0.50         | 0.50
0.75               | 375           | 125          | 0.75         | 0.25

At the default split a valid forwarded frame uses 500 units. A CRC-rejected
frame uses 250 units; the remaining relay allocation is unused. The source
and relay transmit in different slots. Insert partA_timing.png and
partA_energy.png. These splits demonstrate configuration, not an optimum.

4. DECODE-AND-FORWARD
The relay detects bits from the signs of equalized QPSK symbols, checks the CRC,
then remodulates the detected frame and scales it to relay power if CRC passes.
On failure, the phase-2 output is all zeros. This is uncoded decode-and-forward
with error detection; CRC neither corrects errors nor detects every error pattern.
Relay forwarding belongs to Part A under the original assignment; the receiver
member should reuse this interface when integrating the group's system.

5. VALIDATION
Ideal test: recover every full frame and all 97,600 payload bits exactly.
Expected and checked results: zero payload errors and 100/100 CRC passes.
Demodulating the forwarded symbols must also recover the complete frames.
A separate CRC test deliberately flips one payload bit in frame 1. It must
reject that frame and forward the other 99. This is a controlled functional
test, not a wireless success rate or measured BER/PER result.
The script also checks unit symbol energy, nonoverlapping slots and the
per-frame energy budget for each allocation in the table.
See partA_validation.txt for the MATLAB results.

6. DESIGN CHOICE AND HANDOFF
Choose an equal split as an initial configuration and CRC-based forwarding to
suppress detected corrupted packets. The group must assess the best split
after integrating its channel, receiver and analysis modules.
Use the same energy budget per attempted payload frame for direct/cooperative
comparisons. Energy per successfully delivered payload is a later metric.
The full group's noiseless test with its agreed receiver remains necessary.
This module's local ideal test verifies the specified mapping and frame format.

RUN AND FILE GUIDE
Keep all five .m files together and run partA_transmitter.m.
transmitter.m: reusable source function; tx=transmitter() uses defaults.
relay_decode_forward.m: Part A forwarding function, taking equalized symbols.
qpsk_codec.m and crc8_bits.m: shared mapping and CRC definitions.
partA_transmitter.m: evidence generation and separate validation cases.
partA_data.mat: tx, idealRelay and crcTestRelay structs; tests are separated.
Four PNG plots, a power allocation CSV, and validation text are regenerated.

INTEGRATION CONTRACT
Signals are complex symbols, not oversampled waveforms. Columns are frames.
tx.sourceTx contains phase-1 symbols with source power already applied.
relay_decode_forward(equalizedSymbols,tx) expects channel phase/amplitude
equalization supplied by the group. Do not feed the raw fading signal into it.
Its relayTx output has relay power already applied; do not scale it twice.
TwoPhase arrays have 1000 rows: phase 1 then phase 2 for each frame column.
tx.payloadRows identifies payload positions; CRC protects header plus payload.
The group must agree on this frame format and rerun the noiseless test with
its receiver. This work does not implement the propagation or destination model.
